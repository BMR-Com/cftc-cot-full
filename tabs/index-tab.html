🏦 Index traders (CIT supplemental report)
Commodity index traders are the passive long-only allocators (BCOM / S&P GSCI trackers, pension and sovereign mandates). The CFTC reports them for 13 agricultural markets only. Their contracts change in January when the indices reweight, then follow money moving into or out of commodities as a whole. Watch breadth: index money added across most markets at once is an allocation flow; one market alone is usually a reweight or a price effect.
