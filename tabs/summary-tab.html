COT index = where funds' net position sits between its low (0) and high (100) over the look-back. Flow σ = this week's net change vs the typical weekly change over 3 years. Driver splits the change into new buying / short covering / long liquidation / fresh shorting. P/M %ile = producer/merchant (commercial) net percentile — commercials at the opposite extreme to funds is a classic divergence. Setup (−100…+100) is contrarian: positive = crowded short with commercials long and flow starting to turn up; it is penalised while flow is still running hard in the crowded direction. Flags: 🔥 squeeze fuel · ⚠️ liquidation risk · ↕️ spec/commercial divergence · ⏩ fast repositioning.
